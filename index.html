Somewhere Along The Way

I still remember the day our story truly began - 3 December 2022. Our seniors created a Documentation Team, and both you and I became part of it. That was the day we had our first real conversation. What started as simple communication for our work slowly became something much more meaningful. From that day, we started knowing each other through conversations, laughs, random talks, and all the little moments we shared. Looking back now, I realize that 3 December 2022 wasn't just the day we became teammates. It was the day I met someone who would eventually become one of the most important people in my life.

From Me To You

A Few Things I Want To Say

Dear Priti,

First of all, Happy Birthday! ❤️ I don't think I have ever properly told you how special you are to me, so I wanted to do it here, in my own little way.

We met in college, and honestly, at that time I had no idea that you would eventually become such an important part of my life. What started as simply knowing each other slowly became friendship, then a really close friendship, and somewhere along the way you became my best friend.

But even "best friend" doesn't completely explain what you mean to me. You are one of those people with whom I can be completely myself. We can talk about serious things, stupid things, random things, or sometimes absolutely nothing, and somehow those moments still become memories I want to keep.

I am genuinely thankful for every conversation, every laugh, every stupid joke, every little moment, and even the times when things weren't perfect. All of them became a part of our story, and I wouldn't want to replace any of it.

Today, on your 25th birthday, I just want to wish you a life full of happiness, peace, success, good people, new experiences and everything you truly deserve. I hope this new chapter brings you closer to all the things you dream about.

And wherever life takes us from here, I hope we never become strangers. I hope there are still many birthdays to celebrate, many random conversations to have, many places to go, and many more memories waiting for us.

Thank you for being you, Priti. Thank you for becoming such an important part of my life. And most importantly, thank you for being my friend.

Happy 25th Birthday! 🎂❤️ I hope this little surprise makes you smile, because that is the least I could do for someone who has given me so many reasons to smile.

Always wishing the best for you ❤️
